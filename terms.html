AIRI Second Brain

個人用途 / Personal purpose

這是由個人維護的自用筆記同步專案，將自己授權的 Google Drive 筆記同步到自己的電腦。本網站只提供說明，不提供公眾註冊或代管同步服務。

This individually maintained project syncs the owner's authorized Google Drive notes to the owner's own computer. This website provides information only; it offers no public registration or hosted sync service.

授權與控制 / Authorization and control

僅處理自己有權存取並已授權的資料。使用者自行管理 Google 授權、本機副本與備份；可停止同步或撤銷 Google 存取權。資料處理範圍請見隱私說明。

Use only data you are entitled to access and have authorized. The user manages Google permissions, local copies, and backups, and may stop sync or revoke Google access. See the privacy notice for the scope of this workflow.

專案範圍 / Project scope

本頁說明個人同步用途，不提供公眾代管服務或服務水準承諾，也不表示任何第三方認證。

This page describes a personal sync workflow. It offers no public hosted service or service-level commitment and makes no third-party certification claims.
