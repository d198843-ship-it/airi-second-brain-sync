個人筆記同步工具 · Personal note sync workflow

AIRI Second Brain

把自己的 Google Drive 筆記，同步到自己的電腦。

A personal workflow for reading authorized Google Drive notes and syncing them to the owner's own computer.

用途 / Purpose

這是個人維護的自用專案。透過 rclone 與自有 Google OAuth 用戶端，讀取使用者授權的 Google Drive 筆記並同步到自己的電腦，供個人使用。

This individually maintained project uses rclone and a personal Google OAuth client to read the user's authorized Google Drive notes and sync them to the user's own computer for personal use.
